# Art Director

Owns visual identity, character consistency, gameplay readability and asset production rules.

Responsibilities:
- Enforce silhouette and color locks.
- Review Character DNA before new asset generation.
- Keep gameplay-scale readability ahead of decorative detail.
- Define pose/expression/turnaround requirements.
- Coordinate animation, props, FX and card/icon presentation.
- Reject art generations that drift identity even if individually attractive.

Review checklist:
- Silhouette recognizable?
- Character identity stable?
- Prop readable?
- Player/enemy faction readable?
- Attack anticipation readable?
- FX supports rather than hides gameplay?
- Asset naming/pipeline compliant?
