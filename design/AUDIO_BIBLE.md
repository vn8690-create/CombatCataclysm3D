# Combat Cataclysm 3D — Audio Bible

## Audio goal
Sound should make impacts funnier, characters more memorable, and major comedy events instantly recognizable.

## Priority order
1. Combat readability
2. Character identity
3. Comedy timing
4. Musical flavor
5. Quantity

## Character voice package
Major characters may include:
- Spawn lines
- Attack grunts
- Skill calls
- Hidden-skill lines
- Friendly-fire reactions
- Rival/friend reactions
- Low-HP reactions
- Victory lines
- KO lines

Do not make every action talk. Silence preserves punchlines.

## SFX language
- Normal hit: short and dry
- Heavy hit: low transient + body impact
- Knockback: impact + whoosh
- Wrong Syringe: tiny medical cue followed by absurd reaction
- Manager Cannon: grab cue, launch whoosh, impact, crowd sting
- SASHIMI: hook cue, freeze sting, comedic slice accent
- Boss intro: identity sting, not generic explosion spam

## Comedy timing
The sound cue may arrive slightly before, exactly on, or shortly after an action depending on the joke. Audio timing is part of the gag and should be authored with animation/camera timing.

## Music
Country/region music should suggest place without leaning on caricature. Battle music must leave space for event stingers and voice lines.

## Mix rules
- Major events temporarily duck background music
- Repeated unit attacks must be variance-safe and non-fatiguing
- UI sounds stay lighter than battle impacts
- Boss telegraphs must remain audible in crowded battles

## Asset naming
Use deterministic names such as:
- voice_gym_uncle_spawn_01
- sfx_manager_cannon_launch
- sfx_sashimi_hit
- music_japan_stage_01
- sting_hidden_skill
