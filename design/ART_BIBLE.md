# Combat Cataclysm 3D — Art Bible

## Visual identity
Combat Cataclysm should look readable first and ridiculous second. Characters must be identifiable at gameplay scale before detail is added.

## Character shape language
- Large head and expressive face
- Exaggerated hands/props
- Small or comedic legs when appropriate
- Strong silhouette that remains readable at small scale
- One dominant visual gag per character
- Avoid realistic anatomy unless the joke depends on it

## Style lock
- Stylized 3D cartoon presentation
- Bold, clean forms
- Saturated but controlled palette
- Strong value separation between player, enemy and environment
- Avoid visual noise around the combat lane
- Props and costumes carry personality

## Animation principles
- Squash and stretch
- Anticipation before big attacks
- Clear contact pose
- Short hit-stop on important impacts
- Recovery pose that sells personality
- Exaggerated knockback for comedy
- Reactions are part of the joke

## Character asset package
Each canonical character should eventually have:
1. Character DNA JSON
2. Front/side/back turnaround
3. 8-angle reference sheet
4. Expression sheet
5. Combat pose sheet
6. Color palette
7. Prop sheet
8. Animation notes
9. Skill FX notes
10. Icon and card portrait

## Gameplay animation set
Minimum target per major unit:
- Idle
- Move
- Attack
- Skill
- Hit
- Knockback
- KO/death
- Victory
- Taunt/reaction

## Camera-readability constraints
- Avoid attacks whose windup cannot be read at normal zoom
- Important FX should not hide hit targets for long
- Friendly-fire accidents must be visually telegraphed
- Boss attacks require stronger silhouettes and warning language

## AI asset rule
AI-generated art is reference material until it passes silhouette, color, pose, and identity checks. Do not let a new generation silently redesign a character.
