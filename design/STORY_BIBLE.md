# Combat Cataclysm 3D — Story & Bōken Bible

## Purpose
The adventure layer exists to give battles context, discovery, pacing and character encounters. It should not replace the fast lane-defense core.

## Player fantasy
Travel the world with a growing army of unstable weirdos. Every new region can introduce a new recruit, rival, enemy, absurd local problem or secret encounter.

## Bōken structure
- World map travel
- Region hubs
- Short exploration scenes
- NPC conversations
- Side quests
- Recruitment events
- Shops/collectibles
- Surprise battles
- Hidden routes
- Boss encounters

## Quest philosophy
Quests should be short, memorable and mechanically connected to battle systems.

Examples:
- Recover a supermarket bag stolen during a street fight
- Protect Gym Uncle's ridiculous outdoor gym
- Find Otaku Supreme's missing figure
- Escort Drunk Uncle home without letting him start three unrelated fights
- Help Fisherman Uncle catch the 'wrong shark'

## Tone
Comedy first, but characters can still have attachment, rivalry, embarrassment, pride and small emotional arcs. Avoid lore walls. Let action, animation and short dialogue carry personality.

## Narrative delivery
Preferred order:
1. Visual situation
2. Character reaction
3. Short punchline
4. Battle or interaction
5. Consequence

## World continuity
Recurring characters remember major relationships and comedy incidents. Hidden skills and rivalries discovered in battle can unlock adventure dialogue or side events.

## Scope control
Bōken is a later-layer system. Do not begin full exploration implementation until the battle vertical slice is stable and enjoyable.
