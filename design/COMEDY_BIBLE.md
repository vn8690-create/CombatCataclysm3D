# Combat Cataclysm 3D — Comedy Bible

## Prime directive
The battlefield should generate stories, not just damage numbers.

## Comedy hierarchy
1. Character behavior
2. Animation and timing
3. Unexpected interaction
4. Camera emphasis
5. Short punchline text

## Rules
- Do not spam dialogue bubbles or floating text.
- Do not explain every joke.
- Use anticipation, pause, impact and reaction.
- Reserve freeze frame, zoom, spotlight and slow motion for genuinely important beats.
- Rare events should feel discovered, not scheduled.
- Character relationships may override optimal combat behavior when the result is funny and still readable.

## Comedy event categories
- Hidden skill
- Character combo
- Counter payoff
- Wrong action / failed support
- Kidnap / drag-away event
- Boss humiliation
- Ally rivalry
- Fear reaction
- Crowd reaction
- Environmental gag

## Event examples
- Manager Cannon
- Wrong Syringe
- SASHIMI
- Emergency Meeting
- Human Baseball
- Protein Rage
- Enemy Kidnap Event

## Director timing
A future ComedyDirector should own:
- camera zoom
- hit stop
- spotlight
- freeze frame
- short slow motion
- temporary UI suppression
- reaction timing

Only one major comedy beat should dominate attention at a time.
