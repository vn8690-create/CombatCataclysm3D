# Combat Cataclysm 3D — World Tour Bible

## Long-term fantasy
The campaign eventually becomes a comedy world tour: the player travels through regions and countries, recruits bizarre characters, encounters local bosses, and keeps discovering new battle rules.

## Country-pack template
A mature region may contain:
- 15–20 regular characters/enemies
- 3–5 elite encounters
- 2 major bosses
- 1 hidden boss/event
- 3–6 stage backgrounds
- Local props/hazards
- Exploration encounters
- Region-specific comedy mechanics

## Cultural rule
Use recognizable places, jobs, habits, foods, transport, weather, architecture and everyday situations as inspiration. Humor should target situations and archetypes, not demean nationalities, ethnicities or religions.

## Example directions
### Japan
Salaryman pressure, convenience stores, commuter chaos, otaku culture, neighborhood oddballs, corporate bosses.

### Vietnam
Motorbike chaos, street food, neighborhood characters, beer-table comedy, delivery culture, family archetypes.

### Australia
Wildlife, beach culture, heat, oversized outdoor threats, sports and survival absurdity.

### France
Cooking, fashion, cafés, bureaucracy, dramatic culinary rivalry.

### Italy
Food rivalry, family theatrics, scooters, dramatic gestures, old-city environments.

## World map structure
World -> Country/Region -> Route -> Stage Node -> Battle/Encounter -> Reward/Discovery.

Nodes may include:
- Main battle
- Elite battle
- Boss
- Side quest
- Shop
- Recruitment event
- Treasure/collectible
- Hidden route
- Comedy event

## Production rule
Do not build the whole planet before the core battle loop is polished. Country expansion starts only after Combat Feel, Comedy Director, Camera, Spotlight, Animation and the Character Factory are stable.
